Our Story

Hello, I'm Krista Miller.

I founded Fettle Fitt after leaving a career supporting my community through Meals on Wheels — a path that taught me how much people thrive when someone shows up for them consistently. I wanted to bring that same spirit into fitness.

"Fettle Fitt is more than just workouts; it's a community where women can feel supported, celebrated, and challenged."

My goal is simple: help every woman who walks through our doors build strength, confidence, and a healthier mindset — inside and out. I'm so excited to be part of your journey.
